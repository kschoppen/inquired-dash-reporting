Contact - IJ Nurture Flow 2627 Highest volume
4,662 delivered · 717 suppressed · 24.4% open · 2.4% click · 0.43% unsubscribe · 8 of 17 emails sending. Both tracks are running: Leadership modules 1 and 2 (1,060 and 1,103 delivered) and Classroom modules 1 and 2 (1,249 and 1,216). Module 3 has barely started, 12 sends on LT and 2 on CT. Open rate drops from about 28% on module 1 to about 21% on module 2 in both tracks, which is a normal second-touch settle, not a warning. The thing worth explaining is downstream: this program carries 78% of portfolio delivery and has produced exactly one lifecycle stage move.
Contact - Inkwell Nurture Flow 2627 Best engagement
892 delivered · 52 suppressed · 29.3% open · 2.7% click · 1.01% unsubscribe · 7 of 16 emails sending. The strongest engagement in the portfolio on both open and click, and the lowest suppression rate by a wide margin (6% of delivered, against 15% portfolio-wide). Leadership module 1 opens at 33.0%, the best single result anywhere. Unsubscribe is just over the 1% guardrail at 1.01% (9 of 892), worth watching rather than acting on. Inkwell also accounts for 14 of the 15 lifecycle moves since launch.
Contact - GF8 Nurture Flow 2627 Thin sample
236 delivered · 35 suppressed · 22.0% open · 0.8% click · 1.27% unsubscribe · 4 of 6 emails sending. Only Leadership modules 1 and 2 have real volume (120 and 112); modules 3 and 4 show 2 sends each, which are tests. Clicks are 2 across the whole program, so no click read is possible yet. Unsubscribe reads 1.27%, above the guardrail, but that is 3 people out of 236 and too thin to act on. GF8 still has no contact-level nurture tracking, so none of it reaches the lifecycle section above.
Contact - Agnostic Nurture Flow 2627 Suppression outlier
150 delivered · 112 suppressed · 16.0% open · 0.7% click · 0 unsubscribes · 4 of 6 emails sending. Suppression is the story: 112 suppressed against 150 delivered, a 75% ratio against 15% portfolio-wide. Two of the four sending emails suppress more than they deliver (module 2: 23 delivered, 30 suppressed; module 3: 22 delivered, 26 suppressed). Open rate is also the lowest of any program at 16.0%, and module 3 sits at 4.5%. Worth checking the suppression lists and frequency caps on this track before reading anything else into the engagement numbers. The cause is not confirmable from the API.
Contact - WH Nurture Flow 2627 Not sending
0 delivered · 0 suppressed · no data on any of its 4 emails. Unchanged across every check since Aug 31. All four World History emails exist and carry the correct nurture property, but none has ever sent. Two possible causes, neither confirmable through the API: the enrollment audience is empty, or the workflow is not switched on. This program also lacks the nurture_track__world_history contact property entirely, re-checked in HubSpot Sep 22, so it has an enrollment list but no active or exited tracking lists and cannot appear in the lifecycle section above.

  • ●The Aug 3 launch date used in earlier versions of this page was wrong; the program went live Aug 18. Every lifecycle figure above has been recalculated from Aug 18.   • ●Nothing has sent past module 2 in any program. 26 of 49 registered emails have no send data; the handful showing 1 or 2 sends are tests. Five weeks post-launch on a spaced cadence, this is expected rather than broken, but it means every engagement rate on this page describes modules 1 and 2 only. Re-check once module 3 is genuinely in flight.
  • ●Agnostic suppresses more than it delivers on half its live emails. Program-wide, 112 suppressed against 150 delivered (75%, against 15% portfolio-wide), and modules 2 and 3 each individually suppress more than they send. Worth checking that track’s suppression lists and frequency caps. The cause cannot be confirmed through the API.
  • ●World History has still never sent an email, unchanged since Aug 31: 0 delivered across all 4 emails. Either the enrollment audience is empty or the workflow is not live, and neither is readable via API.   • ●GF8 and Agnostic still have essentially no contact-level nurture tracking despite actively sending (236 and 150 delivered). Nothing in the lifecycle section can include them. Worth comparing against what Tim’s reporting rebuild finds.
  • ●Inkwell’s unsubscribe rate is 1.01% (9 of 892) and GF8’s is 1.27% (3 of 236), both fractionally over the under-1% program guardrail. Both are thin enough that one or two people move the number. Watch, do not act yet.

  • ●Confirm enabled/paused state and branch logic for all 5 flows directly in HubSpot. Still not readable via API.
